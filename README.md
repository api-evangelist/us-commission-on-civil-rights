The US Commission on Civil Rights is an independent, bipartisan federal agency that investigates, reports on, and issues public service announcements about discrimination or denials of equal protection based on race, color, religion, sex, age, disability, national origin, or in the electoral process. The Commission informs the development of national civil rights policy and laws through factual research, advisory committees in all 50 states, and public reporting to the President and Congress.

## Features

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Civil Rights Complaints Data | Interactive map and datasets of civil rights complaints filed with federal agencies, organized by state and subject matter. |
| Data Inventory Program | Annual comprehensive data inventory maintained per the Foundations for Evidence-Based Policymaking Act of 2018, with a designated Chief Data Officer. |
| No FEAR Act Statistics | Federal employee discrimination complaint statistics required under the Notification and Federal Employee Antidiscrimination and Retaliation Act. |
| Advisory Committee Reports | Reports from 51 state and local advisory committees covering civil rights conditions across all 50 states and the District of Columbia. |
| Statutory Enforcement Reports | Congressional and Presidential reports on federal agency civil rights enforcement activities and compliance. |

## Use Cases

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Civil Rights Policy Research | Accessing Commission reports, briefing papers, and findings to inform civil rights policy development and legislative analysis. |
| Discrimination Complaint Analysis | Analyzing civil rights complaint data by state, subject, and federal agency to understand discrimination patterns and trends. |
| Federal Agency Compliance Monitoring | Reviewing No FEAR Act statistics and statutory enforcement data to assess federal agency compliance with civil rights laws. |
| Academic Research | Using Commission datasets and reports for academic research on civil rights, equal opportunity, and discrimination in the United States. |
